What is EQUA?

Recovery, supported in real time.

The Problem

A typical IOP week, like Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 9:30 to 2, is about 13 hours of care. The other 150-plus hours happen on your own. That's where the hard moments hit, and care teams usually don't see them until the next session.

What EQUA Does
✅
Daily Check-In
Mood, sleep, cravings, validated scales (PHQ-9, AUDIT-C, GAD-7) reviewed by your care team.
🆘
Distress Check
Patient flags a craving or crisis in 3 taps. Your clinician sees how it went at the next review.
📈
Progress Tracking
Days sober, session attendance, outcome scores, one view for both patient and provider.
📋
Aftercare Plan
Goals, appointments, and medications, always in the patient's pocket, never lost in a binder.
Built From the Inside

EQUA was designed by a patient in IOP, built on what was actually missing between sessions, not what looked good on a slide deck.

Currently a prototype

This demo is the beginning of a conversation about what recovery support could look like. Your clinical input shapes what gets built next.
