What is EQUA?
EQUA

Recovery, supported in real time.

The Problem

Patients spend 3–4 hours a week in IOP or PHP — and 165 hours on their own. Critical moments happen between sessions, invisible to care teams until it's too late.

What EQUA Does
✅
Daily Check-In
Mood, sleep, cravings — validated scales (PHQ-9, AUDIT-C, GAD-7) surfaced to your team in real time.
🆘
Distress Check
Patient flags a craving or crisis in 3 taps. Clinician sees a timestamped alert before the next session.
📈
Progress Tracking
Days sober, session attendance, outcome scores — one view for both patient and provider.
📋
Aftercare Plan
Goals, appointments, and medications — always in the patient's pocket, never lost in a binder.
Built From the Inside

EQUA was designed by a patient in IOP at Old Vineyard Behavioral Health — built on what was actually missing between sessions, not what looked good on a slide deck.

Currently a prototype

This demo is the beginning of a conversation about what recovery support could look like. Your clinical input shapes what gets built next.

1–3 · Low Distress
Uncomfortable but manageable. Stay in your routine.
🎧Put on music — something that shifts the vibe, not amplifies it
🚶Walk outside for 10 minutes, no destination
📓Write 3 sentences in your journal about what's bothering you
💊Take your Naltrexone if you haven't yet today
4–7 · Moderate Distress
This is the zone where the plan really matters. Don't white-knuckle it alone.
📞Reach out to your peer support contact — even saying "I'm struggling" out loud helps
🫁Do 4-7-8 breathing for 4 full cycles before anything else
❄️Hold cold water on your wrists for 60 seconds — Dr. Rivera's #1 for you
📱Text Dr. Rivera if it doesn't drop below a 4 within 20 minutes

What you can do in Equa's Clinical View
📋 Track outcomes — PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT-C, and C-SSRS scores populate automatically as patients complete check-ins.
⚡ Real-time alerts — Care flags surface the moment a patient's distress score or instrument score crosses a clinical threshold.
🗂 Distress plans — Pre-load a personalized 3-tier crisis plan for any patient. They see it in their app the moment they rate distress.
📲 Push updates — Edit a patient's plan anytime and push it live to their app without them needing to do anything.
👁 360° patient view — Tap any patient to see their full chart: check-in history, assessment scores, distress ratings, and plan status.
All data is HIPAA-compliant. Equa does not store PHI without a signed BAA. · Old Vineyard Behavioral Health · Equa v1.0 Beta
