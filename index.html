EQUA
Built for the moments when you can't build anything.
The problem
Recovery plans fail at the worst times.
When you're in crisis, your prefrontal cortex — the part that makes plans — goes offline. You can't access what you know. EQUA is built for that exact moment.
The solution
A plan built when you're clear. Used when you're not.
You build your recovery plan during a calm moment — your values, your warning signs, your support contacts. When crisis hits, EQUA walks you through it step by step.
The science
Every intervention is evidence-based.
Box breathing, physiological sighs, grounding, Opposite Action — all DBT and neuroscience-backed protocols. Not wellness fluff. Real tools that work on your nervous system.
EQUA
Three steps. No thinking required.
Step 01
Build your plan — once.
Answer a few questions about what you're protecting, your warning signs, and your support network. Takes about 5 minutes. You only do this when you're calm and clear.
Step 02
When crisis hits — tap "I need help."
One button. EQUA identifies what's happening — craving, shutting down, escalating, or overwhelmed — and gives you the right intervention for that state.
Step 03
Follow the steps. One at a time.
Each intervention is broken into timed, guided steps. You don't have to think — just do the next thing. When you're through, EQUA checks in and logs the outcome.

Shutting Down · Step 1 of 3
Step 1 of 3 · 30 seconds
Cold water on your face or wrists.
The freeze response is physiological. Cold water activates the dive reflex and directly interrupts the shutdown. Do this first, before anything else.

Overwhelm is your brain processing too many inputs at once. The goal is not to solve everything — it's to reduce the load so you can act on one thing.
Overwhelmed · Step 1 of 4
Step 1 of 4 · 2 minutes
Name what you can sense. Right now.
Say out loud: 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. This interrupts the cognitive spiral by anchoring your nervous system to the present moment.

For Clinicians
Behavioral Health Technology
Real-time patient visibility between sessions.
EQUA gives your IOP and PHP patients a structured crisis tool — and gives you a window into how they're actually doing between check-ins.
How it helps your practice
📊
Real-time crisis visibility
See when a patient activates a crisis pathway, what intervention they used, and whether their distress level dropped. No more waiting until next session to find out.
📋
Daily check-in data
Patients answer the same questions you ask in group — safety, sleep, medications, mood — and you see the trend over time, not just today's answer.
🧭
Discharge continuity
The plan built during treatment travels with them. What you co-create in IOP doesn't disappear on discharge day — it becomes their tool on day one.
