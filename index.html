Clinician Review
For behavioral health professionals
Help me build this responsibly.
EQUA is an experimental recovery-navigation concept. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and it does not replace therapy, psychiatry, medication, AA/peer support, or emergency services.

I'm a patient in IOP at Old Vineyard. I built this because I've experienced what it's trying to solve: knowing what to do and being unable to execute it when activated.

I'd rather you find the holes now.
